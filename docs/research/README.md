# Research records

Store scoped research as `YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md`. Read `AGENTS.md` and the Research Architect role before contributing.

Each record should include:

- Question, scope, and task goal.
- Observed repository facts and approved requirements.
- Sources with links, access dates, and relevant jurisdiction or version.
- Findings, uncertainty, assumptions, and alternatives.
- Business-critical questions and required human decisions.
- Recommendation, clearly labeled as a proposal.
- Links to architecture, ADRs, and the task record.

Figma and prototype behavior provide design context; neither establishes approved marketplace policy.
