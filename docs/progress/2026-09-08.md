## Known limitations

The homepage remains illustrative. Search updates the summary, not candidate filtering. Existing weight/volume limits and string inputs remain presentation behavior, not production capacity policy. No airport consumers exist; route fields remain in SearchValues and sample Trip rather than introducing an unused Route abstraction or physical-unit model. Country/city pair validation and production date/time semantics remain future work. The date formatter remains in the sample module with its current consumers. Browser verification used installed Chrome only; other browser engines were not tested.

Match Score observation: the Figma-derived UI displays 94%, 89% and 86%; the sample trip scores also include 78. The domain model's MVP matching section specifies deterministic ranking with explainable reasons, not probability-style percentages. This is a known product inconsistency, recorded without changing scores, labels or UI. Future product wording/ranking changes need their own authorization.
